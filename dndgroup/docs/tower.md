I(initiative) - Initiative tracks time. After acting, add the action's I cost to your initiative. The combatant with the lowest initiative takes the next turn.

## **The Treant**

_Thorn Tank_

**Base Stats:** HP 40 |
### **Passive: Stand Tall**

You don't lose block at the start of each turn, and, if you have over 20 block, gain 1 thorn. Block can accumulate indefinitely and enhances your thorny counterattacks.
Upon being attacked deal damage back to the attacker equal to your current thorns

### **TREE!!**

- Cost: 10I -2 Thorn 
- Effect: 2 Range | 2 Damage | Inflict +12 I 
- Description: Your signature root spell. Locks down a distant enemy, preventing them from moving. Trade thorns for control. 

### **Thorn in your side**

- Cost: 15I 
- Effect: 4 Range | 10 Damage (+1 per Thorn you have) 
- Description: Scales with your thorns, making this your primary damage output. Incentivizes tanking hits and building stacks.

### **Tree Hugger**

- Cost: 10I
- Effect: 12 Block
- Trigger: Each time you are attacked before your next turn, gain 1 Thorn 
- Description: A reactive ability. The more enemies gang up on you, the stronger you become. Encourages enemies to focus fire or leave you alone.

### **Shrapnel**

- Cost: 24I, cost 3I less for each Thorn you use, (max 8)
- Effect: 2 AOE range | 15 Damage
- Description: Explode your thorns for area damage. Use when you've built up stacks or need to clear trash.

### **Nature's Jewels**

- Cost: 10I
- Effect: 3 AOE range | Pull >1 enemy | Apply Taunt
- Description: Targets focus on you until attacked by a different character. Master aggro control ability—forces enemies to come to you.

### **Reinforcing Roots**

- Cost: 15I
- Effect: +18 Block | For each adjacent character, gain +1 additional Block
- Description: Stack this with allies to become nearly unkillable. Position strategically near your team for massive defensive value.

### **Growth**

- Cost: 5I
- Effect: Remove all block and gain 1 Thorn for each 5 Block removed | Deal damage equal to thorns gained in 3 AOE
- Description: Sacrifice your defense for offense. Converts accumulated block into thorn stacks. Converts your HP sponge into damage output when needed.

## **The Tactician**

_Spear Sentinel_

**Base Stats:** HP 32

### **Passive: Superior Positioning**

For each enemy 2 hexagons away from you, actions cost -1 Initiative. Position yourself as the hub in enemy territory to reduce your ability costs dramatically. 

### **Thrust**

- Cost: 12I
- Effect: 9 Damage | 2 Range
- Description: Your reliable ranged attack. Use when enemies are at medium distance to maintain control without getting close.

### **Anticipate**

- Cost: 10I
- Effect: +10 Block | If attacked before your next turn, gain -3I (reduction)
- Description: Defensive positioning spell. Cheaper future actions if enemies dare attack you while you're guarded.

### **Burst**

- Cost: 6I
- Effect: >2 in a line (line-of-sight, close range)
- Description: Your cheapest ability for hitting multiple enemies. Forces enemies to spread out or get hit together.

### **Riposte**

- Cost: 8I
- Effect: +8 Block | If attacked before your next turn, deal 6 Damage back to attacker (reflects damage)
- Description: Punish aggressive enemies. The more they focus you, the more they hurt themselves. Passive deterrent.

### **Pin Down**

- Cost: 11I
- Effect: 7 Damage at 3 Range | Inflict +3 Immobilize
- Description: Lock enemies in place from range. Prevents them from closing distance or fleeing. Use to isolate high-priority targets.

### **Formation Mastery**

- Cost: 14I
- Effect: Gain +1 Block for each adjacent ally | Gain -2I cost for each adjacent enemy (stacking)
- Description: The ultimate positioning ability. Position yourself between allies and enemies to become exponentially more efficient. Works best in chaotic fights.

### **Spear Wall**

- Cost: 13I
- Effect: +15 Block | Enemies who move into your space take 5 Damage | Lasts until your next turn
- Description: Create a physical barrier. Enemies pay the price for approaching. Cheap-ish for the defensive value when positioned well.

### **Tactical Retreat**

- Cost: 9I
- Effect: Move 2 hexes | If you move away from enemies, gain -1I to your next action
- Description: Reposition yourself strategically. Kiting enemies while they chase is when this shines. Reward for good spacing.

### **Commanding Presence**

- Cost: 12I
- Effect: All allies in 2 Range gain +1 to their next action (cheaper next turn) | Gain +2 Block
- Description: Lead from the field. Your positioning benefits your entire team. Buff gets stronger as you cluster allies.

**Playstyle:** Controls space with superior positioning. Enemies hate fighting this guy because he denies access and makes them pay for getting close. Superior Positioning makes abilities cheaper the more enemies surround you, turning a numbers disadvantage into an advantage. Your kit rewards tight formations and careful spacing—place yourself as the fulcrum between allies and enemies.

## **The Artificer**

_Turrets & Mechs_

**Base Stats:** HP 30 | Scrap Capacity 6 |

### **Passive: Adrenaline Junkie**

Start each combat with 2 Scrap. You may spend 3hp to gain 1 Scrap once per turn. Every global tick, gain 1 Scrap.
This converts health into resources, allowing aggressive mana generation when shielded by constructs.

### **Deploy Turret**

- Cost: 10I -3 Scrap
- Effect: Summon a Turret construct (10 HP)
  - Turret attacks: 12I for 6 Damage at 4 Range
  - Starts with current Initiative +12I
  - Acts independently each turn
- Description: Your foundation. Each turret is a persistent threat that enemies must deal with or ignore (at their peril).

### **Duct Tape**

- Cost: 8I
- Effect: Heal a construct by 8 HP
- Description: Repair your creations mid-battle. Prioritize healing high-threat turrets/walkers to keep them alive longer.

### **Enter Mech**

- Cost: 14I -3 Scrap
- Effect: Summon a Walker mech (20 HP) and enter it
  - Walker attacks: 10I for 15 Damage
  - Starts with current Initiative +10I
  - Can move 1> after attacking (mobile platform)
  - Movement: Can move 1 hex after attacking, making it mobile for a mech
  - If the Walker dies, you take 10 damage and lose 5 Scrap (expensive loss)
  - While the Walker is alive, you cannot be targeted by attacks or enemy actions (invulnerability)
  - You can exit the Walker on your turn, but you cannot take any actions that turn
  - While in the Walker, you cannot take actions that aren't Walker actions, but you can still use your passive and gain Scrap
- Description: Your ultimate survival tool. Sacrifice damage output for near-invincibility. Use when heavily damaged or to coordinate massive attacks.

### **Overclock**

- Cost: 6I -2 Scrap
- Effect: A construct immediately takes an extra turn out of order
- Description: Accelerate your army. Use before big enemy turns to burst them down, or after deploying new units to maximize their impact.

**Playstyle:** Build an army. The battlefield slowly fills with machinery. Early game is fragile, but as you accumulate constructs, you become nearly unkillable. The Walker form provides burst mobility and survivability windows when things get dangerous.

## **The Arsonist**

_Pyromancer Warlock_

**Base Stats:** HP 28 | Movement 2 | Initiative 5

### **Passive: Fuel the Flames**

Whenever Burning deals damage: Heal 1hp. This creates a self-sustaining engine where your damage over time effects keep you alive in extended fights.

### **Firebolt**

- Cost: 12I
- Effect: 10 Damage at 4 Range | Apply Burning 2
- Description: Your basic ranged spell. Reliable damage that feeds into your passive healing. Use to poke enemies safely.

### **Infernal Brand**

- Cost: 10I -1 Spirit
- Effect: Apply Burning 5 (long duration)
- Description: Plant high-duration burn stacks. Cheaper than direct attacks but requires time to pay off. Use early against durable enemies.

### **Flame Burst**

- Cost: 14I
- Effect: 2 AOE range | 8 Damage | Apply Burning 3 to all hit
- Description: Your AOE tool. Clear groups and apply midrange burns.

### **Immolation**

- Cost: 15I
- Effect: 4 Range | Lose 6 HP (self-damage) | Apply Burning 6 to target
- Description: Desperate firepower. Trade your health for massive burn stacks when you're shielded or confident in your survival.

**Playstyle:** Everything burns. Long fights heavily favor the Arsonist because enemies accumulate debuffs while you regain health. Weak early but scales massively over time as burning stacks multiply.

## **The Mask**

_Sensitive_

**Base Stats:** HP 25 | Movement 2 | Initiative 6

Each time you lose hp gain 1 Rage, when you have 10 Rage trigger Broken state for massive damage and control.

### **Breathe**

- Cost: X\*2 Initiative
- Effect: Lose all your Rage (X amount) | Heal 1 HP per Rage spent
- Description: Your panic button. Convert accumulated rage into healing. Use when Broken becomes inevitable and you need to survive.

### **Quiet Time**

- Cost: Toggle Initiative (always costs while active)
- Effect: Each time an action is performed in 4 AOE range, gain 1 Rage and deal 1 Damage to all enemies in area
- Description: Passive area control. Toggle on in congested fights for free value, toggle off when isolated to save Initiative.

### **Sharing is Caring**

- Cost: 5I
- Effect: Lose 5 HP | Gain 10 Block | Give 5 HP to 1 ally of your choice
- Description: Self-sacrifice for team protection. Trade your safety for an ally's. Use on key targets who need to survive.

### **Therapeutic**

- Cost: 15I -4 Rage
- Effect: 16 Damage
- Description: Heal your mental state while dealing damage. Great for cooling down after Broken state when enemies are low.

### **Jab**

- Cost: 10I
- Effect: 8 Damage | Gain +1 Rage
- Description: Your basic attack. Every hit edges you closer to Broken. Use carefully or lean into the chaos.

### **Diving Kick**

- Cost: 16I -2 Rage
- Effect: >2 range | 12 Damage
- Description: Close-range burst attack that cools your rage. Use for gap closing and quick raids.

### **Gaze**

- Cost: Varies (action)
- Effect: Inflict 2 Bleeding on yourself and target | You and target are paralyzed until one of you is attacked
- Trigger: After paralysis wears off, both characters gain Initiative equal to the next Global Tick
- Description: High-risk control spell. Mutually incapacitate threats while buying time for allies.

### **Gift**

- Cost: Varies (action)
- Effect: Choose an enemy (no range limit) | Inflict 4 Insanity on the enemy and 2 Insanity on yourself
- Special Rules:
  - You cannot trigger Broken until the enemy is dead
  - There is no limit to the amount of Rage you can have while Gift is on the enemy
  - Upon enemy death, remove all Insanity on yourself
- Description: Lock onto a high-priority target and go berserk on them. Pair with allies to burst them down before you lose control.

### **Broken**

- Activates upon: 10 Rage (automatic, mandatory)
- Deal 10D in a Wave2 pattern (AOE 2)
- Inflict 2 insanity on yourself
- All actions cost +1 Rage and attacks deal +2D (multiplicative bonus)
- When you run out of rage, remove all insanity on yourself and sleep for 10 Initiative (unable to act)
- Playstyle: A high-risk, high-reward glass cannon. You must choose between staying controlled or letting loose for massive damage.

## **The Spiritcaller**

_Living Totem Shaman_

**Base Stats:** HP 32 | Movement 1 | Totem Slots 4 | Initiative 3

### **Passive: Ancestral Guidance**

Whenever a totem activates: Heal 1 HP. This passive synergizes with all totem abilities, giving you consistent healing as your totems work.

### **Healing Totem**

- Cost: 12I
- Effect: Summon a Totem
  - At each Global Tick: All allies heal 2 HP
  - Scales with number of allies
- Description: Your support engine. Place near allies to keep them alive. Each totem is an independent healing source.

### **Flame Totem**

- Cost: 12I
- Effect: Summon a Totem
  - At each Global Tick: All enemies gain Burning 1
  - Damage scales with totem count
- Description: Persistent chip damage. Multiple flame totems create a rising tide of damage enemies can't escape.

### **Doom Totem**

- Cost: 16I
- Effect: Summon a Totem
  - At each Global Tick: All enemies gain Doom 1
  - Stacks indefinitely
- Description: Your control tool. Build up Doom stacks and watch enemies struggle under the weight of inevitable fate.

### **Spirit Totem**

- Cost: 18I
- Effect: Summon a Totem
  - At the start of ally turns: Gain 1 Spirit (regenerate resources)
  - Scales with number of allies near it
- Description: Your mana battery. Resource generation for your team. Prioritize protecting this totem.

**Playstyle:** Instead of acting constantly, you reshape the battlefield through persistent effects. The longer your totems survive, the stronger your team becomes. You provide support and multiplicative buffs/debuffs rather than direct damage. Protect your totems at all costs.

## **The Tideblade**

_Water Dagger Specialist_

**Base Stats:** HP 26 | Movement 3 | Spirit Capacity 3 | Initiative 6

### **Identity**

- Fast and high initiative
- Mobile with multiple movement options
- Hard to pin down or control
- Manipulates enemy positioning through pulls and repositioning
- Chains attacks together for momentum

### **Passive: Flow State**

Whenever you move during your turn: Gain 1 Block. Maximum once per turn. This encourages constant repositioning and rewards aggressive mobility play.

### **Actions**

#### **Flowing Cut**

- Cost: 6I
- Effect: 5 Damage
- Description: Your basic attack. Fast and cheap. Chain these for consistent pressure while repositioning.

#### **Tidal Step**

- Cost: 5I
- Effect: Move 2 hexes | If you move through enemies, inflict 2 Immobilize to them
- Description: Your gap closer. Move through enemies to lock them down or reposition for safety. Efficient movement tool.

#### **Undertow**

- Cost: 10I
- Effect: 6 Damage | Pull target 1 hex toward you
- Description: Hard CC. Force enemies into unfavorable positions or pull them away from allies.

#### **Riptide Strike**

- Cost: 12I
- Effect: Move 1 hex before attacking | 8 Damage
- Description: Mobile opener. Position yourself before striking. Synergizes with Flow State passive for extra block.

### **Water-Themed Advanced Abilities**

#### **Wave Dash**

- Cost: 8I -1 Spirit
- Effect: Move 4 hexes | Can move through enemies (no stopping)
- Description: Your teleport tool. Engage or disengage from any distance. Build huge block stacks if moving to safety.

#### **Whirlpool**

- Cost: 15I -2 Spirit
- Effect: Choose a hex | All units within 1 hex range are pulled toward it (AOE pull)
- Description: Area control ultimate. Group enemies for your allies to clean up or separate them from key targets.

#### **Crashing Current**

- Cost: 14I -2 Spirit
- Effect: 8 Damage | Push target 3 hexes away
- Description: Knockback control. Create space between allies and threats, or push enemies into traps/hazards.

#### **Mist Form**

- Cost: 12I -2 Spirit
- Effect: Until your next turn: The first attack against you misses (dodge active ability)
- Description: Defensive tool. Use before heavy incoming attacks or when surrounded. Guaranteed dodge once.

#### **Flowing Assault**

- Cost: 15I -2 Spirit
- Effect: Move up to 3 hexes | Deal 5 Damage to every enemy passed through (no targeting required)
- Description: AoE momentum attack. Carve through groups while repositioning. Synergizes with Flow State for block stacking.

### **Signature Ability: Drowning Dance**

- Cost: 18I -3 Spirit
- Effect: Attack up to three different enemies | Each attack: 5 Damage | Move 1 hex between attacks
- Description: Your ultimate combo. Chain attacks between enemies while repositioning. Gain massive block from all the repositioning involved.

### **Soaked Status (Optional)**

- At the start of turn: Gain +X Initiative
- Then Soaked decreases by 1
- Example: Soaked 3 → Start turn: +3I → Soaked 2 → Soaked 1 → Soaked 0 (removed)
- Thematic: Water makes enemies sluggish and heavy
- Use on Tideblade enemies to slow them down

### **Alternative Passive: Like Water**

- After attacking: Move 1 hex
- Encourages aggressive play and constant repositioning
- Use if Flow State feels too passive

## **Strategic Guide & Team Building**

### **Core Mechanics Overview**

**Initiative (I)** - Cost of actions. Lower Initiative = more frequent turns

- Treant (3) and Spiritcaller (3) act slowly but forcefully
- Mask (6) and Tideblade (6) act frequently but with less individual impact
- Sweet spot is 4-5 for balanced characters

**Block (B)** - Damage prevention. Renewable resource on tanks

- Treant specializes in infinite block scaling
- Regenerates/resets differently per character (Treant keeps it, others reset per turn)
- Stacking block with Reinforcing Roots creates unkillable walls when grouped

**Spirit (S)** - Premium resource pool for strongest abilities

- Tideblade, Arsonist, Artificer consume Spirit for ultimate actions
- Limited supply creates meaningful choices
- Spiritcaller's Spirit Totem generates +1 Spirit each turn for all allies

**Thorns (Treant)** - Scaling stacking mechanic

- Gain from tanking hits or Tree Hugger
- Enhance damage via Thorn in Your Side
- Convert to area damage via Growth or Shrapnel

**Rage (Mask)** - Builds from taking damage and actions

- Triggers involuntary Broken state at 10 Rage
- Can be managed via Breathe (heal conversion)
- Gift locks onto targets for unlimited Rage generation

**Burning/Doom** - Persistent field effects

- Arsonist scales with burning count (heals 1 HP per tick)
- Spiritcaller layers Doom stacks for cumulative debuffs
- Multiple sources stack multiplicatively

**Scrap (Artificer)** - Army resource

- Generates via Adrenaline Junkie (trade health)
- Deploys turrets and walkers
- Overflow uses Overclock for extra turns

### **Team Composition Archetypes**

**The Tank & Damage Squad**

- Core: Treant + Arsonist
- Treant holds the line, Arsonist piles on persistent damage
- Weakness: Slow decision speed, immobile

**The Utility Swarm**

- Core: Spiritcaller + Tideblade
- Support bot creates advantages, mobile striker exploits them
- Weakness: Requires coordination

**The Unstoppable Force**

- Core: Artificer alone or Artificer + Mask
- Build an army or go berserk
- Weakness: Fragile mid-game, needs to survive startup

**The Precision Strike**

- Core: Tactician + any ranged hero
- Tactical positioning enables cheaper abilities
- Weakness: Requires careful positioning

### **Ability Selection Tips**

**Early Game (Turns 1-3)**

- Prioritize survivability and setup
- Treant: Build block with Reinforcing Roots or Nature's Jewels
- Artificer: Deploy Turrets to start scaling
- Spiritcaller: Place Healing Totem to stabilize allies
- Tactician: Use Anticipate + Thrust for safe play
- Arsonist: Plant Infernal Brand for future payoff

**Mid Game (Turns 4-8)**

- Transition to offensive pressure
- Treant: Unleash Shrapnel or Growth if stacked
- Artificer: Deploy Walker or Overclock existing units
- Spiritcaller: Add Flame/Doom Totems for board pressure
- Mask: Build Rage safely or use Gift to lock targets
- Tideblade: Combine movement + damage for positioning advantage

**Late Game (Turns 9+)**

- Capitalize on accumulated advantages
- Treant: Massive thorn values make single-target crushing
- Artificer: Army is unkillable fortress, minimal health matters
- Spiritcaller: Stacked totems create insurmountable advantage
- Arsonist: Burning stacks heal you faster than enemies damage you
- Mask: Unleash Broken for massive AoE with full team

### **Synergy Interactions**

**Positioning Synergies**

- Tactician enables Treant's Burst via cost reduction
- Tideblade + Spiritcaller: Mobile striker protects/uses totems
- Tactician + Tideblade: Cheap gap closers for space dominance

**Defensive Synergies**

- Treant + Mask: Tank absorbs hits, Mask converts to damage
- Spiritcaller + Anyone: Healing Totem keeps everyone alive longer
- Mask + Tactician: Rage builds from attacks, Tactician's Anticipate triggers bonus damage

**Offensive Synergies**

- Arsonist + Treant: Burning damage triggers Treant's passive healing (healing battery)
- Artificer + Spiritcaller: Multiple summons benefit from Spirit generation
- Mask + Tideblade: Both have high Initiative, Tideblade sets up Mask kills

**Resource Synergies**

- Artificer + Spiritcaller: Scrap + Spirit generation enables constant ability spam
- Tideblade + Anyone: Movement stacks with Flow State for continuous block generation

### **Common Mistakes to Avoid**

1. **Spreading Aggro Too Thin** - Let your tank hold attention. Mask shouldn't be first to engage.
2. **Wasting Spirit Early** - Save premium abilities for high-value moments.
3. **Ignoring Enemy Positioning** - Your formation matters as much as theirs.
4. **Over-committing to Rage (Mask)** - Sometimes Breathe to heal is better than Broken burst.
5. **Leaving Totems Unguarded** - One dead totem ruins Spiritcaller's entire gameplan.
6. **Not Managing Block** - Treant loses value if block resets too often. Position carefully.
7. **Ignoring Initiative Costs** - High-Initiative abilities are a double-edged sword (weak and slow).

### **Stat Point Allocation Strategy**

**Treant** - Prioritize HP and Block (survivability scales damage output)

- HP 40 + Def 20+ Block 15 = unkillable
- Initiative 3 = acceptable slowness

**Arsonist** - Balanced for longevity

- HP 28 (medium) + Spirit 3+ = sustained ability use
- Initiative 5 = frequent turns for damage stacking

**Artificer** - Medium HP, focus on Scrap generation

- HP 30 (safe) + Scrap capacity 6+
- Initiative 4 = reliable deployment rhythm

**Tideblade** - Maximize Initiative

- Initiative 6+ (act most frequently)
- HP 26 (dodge through mobility)
- Spirit 3+ (fuel mobility abilities)

**Mask** - High HP for Rage safety, Initiative for action economy

- HP 25+ (low but mitigated)
- Initiative 6+ (act often to build Rage safely)

**Spiritcaller** - Balanced stats, prioritize HP for survivability

- HP 32+ (protect your support)
- Initiative 3 (acceptable, totems act frequently)

**Tactician** - Versatile scaling

- HP 32 (reasonable tank potential)
- Initiative 4 (consistent positioning opportunities)
- **Blood Mage** - Spends HP instead of Stamina.
